01CONTEXT

SenBioStat is my own product: conceived, designed and built alone, to make biostatistical analysis accessible without knowing R or Python.

02PROBLEM

Students, researchers and health professionals who need to run a statistical test are often stuck between expensive software (SPSS) and coding it themselves. Few tools speak their language, or price themselves for their market.

03SOLUTION

Upload a dataset, choose a method, get a chart and a plain-language result.

A profile tracks institution, specialty and country, and a monthly quota separates the free tier from the paid ones. Every analysis is saved and can be exported as a PDF report.

04AUTOMATIC INTERPRETATION

A statistic is only useful once it becomes a sentence.

Each method computes the right effect size for the job — Cohen's d, eta-squared, Cramér's V — checks its assumptions (Levene's test, outliers), and turns the result into a sentence ready to quote in a report, in French.

05PRICING

Four tiers, built for the local market.

The free tier — 10 analyses a month, the core tests, PDF export — already runs. The paid tiers, priced in FCFA, are designed and ready to switch on at the official launch.

06MY ROLE

Everything: the statistics themselves, the interface, the charts, the PDF reports and the pricing model.

07STACK

Python/Django/Pandas/NumPy/SciPy/Statsmodels/Lifelines/Matplotlib/WeasyPrint/SQLite

08RESULT

Six complete analysis pipelines, from upload to PDF, with automatic interpretation. The free tier runs locally; the paid tiers are waiting on the official launch.
