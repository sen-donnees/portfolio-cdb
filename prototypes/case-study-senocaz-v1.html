01CONTEXT

SenOcaz is a car marketplace I'm building alone for Senegal: buying, selling and renting vehicles, connecting buyers and renters directly with sellers and owners.

02PROBLEM

Buying or renting a car informally usually means scattered listings, no way to tell a serious seller from a vague one, and for rentals, no way to know if a car is actually free on the dates you want.

03SOLUTION

One place to list, find and book — no password required.

Sellers and owners publish a listing with real details and up to 12 photos; buyers filter by brand, price, year, mileage, fuel and city. Signing in only needs a phone number: a code arrives by WhatsApp first, SMS as a fallback, then a 4-digit PIN for next time.

04TRUST

A listing that gets reported enough times disappears on its own.

Members can report a listing for one of several reasons; three distinct reports — or a single scam report — hide it automatically, pending review. Deleting an account genuinely erases its data: photos, showcase, bookings, not just a hidden flag.

05MY ROLE

Everything technical, alone: the data model, passwordless sign-in, booking logic, moderation, and 352 automated tests.

A CI pipeline checks code style, types, security and dependencies on every change, before any test runs.

06STACK

Python/Django/PostgreSQL/Redis/HTMX/Tailwind CSS/WhatsApp Business API/Orange SMS API

07RESULT

352 tests pass. The core marketplace — listings, passwordless sign-in, bookings — is built and covered by a full CI pipeline. Still in development, not yet deployed.

Data × Code × Product

SenOcaz is mostly product and code, close to Sentazur: a real marketplace with little data science involved — the database is geospatial-ready, but no location-based search is built yet.
