01CONTEXT

A six-month tutored project (September 2022 – February 2023) during my Master 2 at Université Grenoble Alpes, with two other students, supervised by a professor working with bioMérieux.

02PROBLEM

Classifying a microbial sample into a known type is well understood. Recognising that a sample belongs to a type nobody has seen yet — a new subtype — is a different, harder problem.

03SOLUTION

A neural network trained to measure similarity, not just to classify.

Working from mass-spectrometry and genome-sequencing data, the network learns a space where samples of the same type sit close together — and an unfamiliar type stands apart. Validated with a “leave-one-type-out” protocol: one type is hidden during training, then tested as if it were genuinely new.

04MY CONTRIBUTION

The Python implementation of the neural network — for classification and novelty detection — and the leave-one-type-out validation.

05STACK

Python/TensorFlow/Keras/NumPy/Matplotlib

06RESULT

A working prototype, validated with the leave-one-type-out protocol and presented to our supervisor and bioMérieux at the end of the project. Confidential: no report, figures or results published here — described at the same level of detail as on my CV.
