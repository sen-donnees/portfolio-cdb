01CONTEXT

DocuSen is my own product: conceived, designed, built and deployed alone. It now also lives inside YoonuJob, my second product.

02PROBLEM

Invoices, contracts, HR letters, attestations: small businesses redo the same paperwork by hand, in templates that never quite look right. The documents that represent a company should look like they were made with care.

03SOLUTION

One clear form per document type, a live preview, and a clean PDF generated on the server.

An account is required to generate a document. Once signed in, you save your documents, reuse your company profile (logo, address, registration numbers) and share a document through a private link.

04ENGINEERING

27 document types, one engine.

Each type started as hand-written views. A generic engine now lets them migrate one by one to pure configuration, and a startup check fails loudly if a type is declared in one place and forgotten in the other. The catalogue can grow without drifting.
