01CONTEXT

YoonuJob is a company I co-founded with a partner who leads the business side. I lead the technical side: I designed and built the whole platform.

02PROBLEM

Hiring is scattered: offers spread across sites, CVs in inconsistent formats, and employers who also need people on the ground for field missions. YoonuJob puts the three sides of that market in one place.

03SOLUTION

Three roles, one platform.

Candidates build a profile and a CV, then apply. Companies publish offers and review applications. A candidate can also apply to become a field ambassador: once validated by the back office, they take on the missions that companies request. An offer can be handled on the platform or redirect to an external application.

04CV BUILDER

What you see is what you download.

Three templates (Modern, Premium Classic, Creative) and a choice of colour palettes. The live preview and the PDF come from the same template, rendered with WeasyPrint, so they always match.

05BACK OFFICE

A back office built for the platform, not the Django admin.

From one dashboard the team validates companies, ambassadors and mission requests, moderates offers and accounts, assigns ambassadors to missions and follows connections. A global search finds any record, the sidebar counts what needs attention, and destructive actions ask for confirmation. Access is restricted to staff. It runs locally and is in its final stretch before going live.

06ENGINEERING

Built for real users.

Brute-force lockout and rate limiting, sanitised rich text, validated file uploads, and more than 300 automated tests. The Docker build compiles the front-end assets in a first stage, then ships a slim Python image with the system libraries WeasyPrint needs.
