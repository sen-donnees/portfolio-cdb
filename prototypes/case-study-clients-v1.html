Client sites

Two more websites built end to end, for two real clients outside my own products: a professional football club, and a local government.

01 / 02

AJEL de Rufisque

Football club · Senegalese Ligue 1

The official site of a professional football club: news, squad, calendar and results, photo gallery, a merch shop, and a page for sponsors.

Built alone over 136 commits. The shop takes orders without online payment — a deliberate choice for this stage of the club’s digital presence. Already tested live on Render for a demo; currently offline.

Commune of Keur Moussa

Local government · institutional site

The official site of a Senegalese commune made up of several villages: news, agenda, projects, a directory of the villages, partners, and a contact form organized by service for residents.
