01CONTEXT

Sentazur is a private security company I’m co-founding with a colleague, based in Avignon: guarding, patrols, event security, access control, fire-safety agents. My colleague is setting up the business side; I designed and built the public website and the internal back office, alone.

02PROBLEM

Running a security company by hand gets heavy fast: which agent is qualified and free for which site, whose certification is about to expire, which quote was sent and to whom.

03SOLUTION

A public site to be found, a private back office to run the business.

Visitors browse services by sector and city, and request a quote online. Behind login, the “espace pro” turns that request into a numbered quote (PDF), a client and a site, a mission, and an agent assignment — with a full change history on every record.

04COMPLIANCE

An agent without a valid certification can’t legally work a site.

Each agent’s qualifications and documents — like the SSIAP fire-safety certification — carry a validity date. The system raises an alert automatically before one expires, instead of someone finding out on site.

05MY ROLE

Everything technical: the public site, the back office, the quote and PDF system, and 102 automated tests.

My colleague is setting up the business side of the company.

06STACK

Python/Django/PostgreSQL/HTML/CSS/Tailwind CSS/WeasyPrint

07RESULT

The public site and the core of the back office — clients, quotes and staff — are already running, covered by 102 tests. Scheduling is being built now, with billing, reporting and the rest of the roadmap next, before going live.

Data × Code × Product
